Privacy notice

Prototype notice · Last updated November 3, 2026

T-chatter is currently a user-interface prototype. This preview does not provide live accounts, send or store messages, upload media, or make calls.

Account and sign-in

Google sign-in is not yet connected. No Google account information is collected by this prototype. When authentication is added, the app will explain what profile data is processed and why.

Messages and calls

Live messaging, voice notes, and voice/video calling are not available yet. Do not use this prototype to share private or sensitive information.

Administrator access

The intended admin console is limited to authentication delivery/health status. Admins must not have access to OTP codes or private message and call content.

Security and encryption

Security controls and end-to-end encryption have not yet been implemented. T-chatter does not claim that this prototype is secure for private communication.

Contact

A contact channel for privacy requests will be published before production launch.
