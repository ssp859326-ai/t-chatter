T-chatter

Privacy notice

Messaging MVP · Last updated November 7, 2026

T-chatter is an early-stage messaging MVP. Use it only with people you know. Do not share passwords, financial details, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information.

Account information

Google sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth when the Google provider has been configured. T-chatter stores the authenticated user's account ID, email address, display name, and profile image URL in its Supabase database to support account and chat features.

Messages

Messages you send are stored in the T-chatter Supabase database and are visible to members of that conversation. Chat access is controlled with database Row Level Security policies.

Email-based contact lookup

To start a one-to-one conversation, a signed-in user enters the other user's registered email address. Profile records are currently visible to signed-in T-chatter users to support this lookup. Use an email address you are comfortable sharing with other signed-in users.

Calls, media, and updates

Voice/video calls, voice notes, media uploads, and Updates/Stories are not available in this release.

Encryption and administrator access

Messages are protected by authenticated access policies, but end-to-end encryption has not been implemented or independently reviewed. Do not assume that messages are end-to-end encrypted. This MVP does not provide an admin dashboard for reading conversations.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database, and realtime infrastructure. Google handles the Google sign-in flow.

Contact

A dedicated privacy contact channel will be added before a wider public launch.
